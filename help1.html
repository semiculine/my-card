Help & Support

Accessibility Support

This page offers tips for using the site with different access needs. You can navigate the page using a keyboard, screen reader, or browser zoom.

Keyboard navigation

Press Tab to move through links and controls, and press Enter to activate a link or button. A visible outline marks the currently focused control.

Making content easier to read

Use your browser’s zoom controls to enlarge the page. Headings and labeled navigation help you find and move between sections.

Need more help?

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please share what page you were using and what happened with the site owner.

Frequently asked questions

How can I use the site without a mouse?

Press Tab to move through links and controls, Shift+Tab to move backward, and Enter or Space to activate the focused control.

Can I make the text larger?

Yes. Use your browser’s zoom controls to enlarge the page while keeping the content and controls available.

How do I find the main sections?

Use the page headings to navigate by section, or use the main navigation links at the top of the page to move between pages.
